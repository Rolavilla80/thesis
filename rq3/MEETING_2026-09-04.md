## Results (the five things to show)
1. REPLICATION EXACT (RQ3.1): 12 languages x 5 configs on a laptop. Unconstrained
   bytes mean 5,192.5 ~= paper's 5,193; _cb mean 182.75 ~= 183; Thai 42,831 digit-
   exact; SCRIPT+_cb = 0 everywhere.
2. WHAT THE JUNK IS (RQ3.2a): 100.00% of 58,308 "mixed" junk tokens are FRAME-SHIFTED
   character spans (one/two misaligned edges). New mechanism + new outlier the paper
   misses: Hebrew (15,510 junk; the whole alphabet shares lead byte D7; the FIRST
   merge of training is already malformed '20 d7'). Law: cascade ignites where the
   atomic grid concentrates (Hebrew D7, Thai E0 B8), stays subcritical where diluted
   (Arabic D8/D9 -> 101). Crucial test: under SCRIPT, Arabic letters share ONE block
   atom -> cascade (18,109; 32.4 violations/1,000 in use). Encoding chooses the
   victims; the constraint stops the disease.
3. CODEPOINT INTEGRITY IS NOT ENOUGH (RQ3.2b): under _cb, decodable tokens still cut
   graphemes (ก|ิ, क्|ष). Types != damage: Hebrew has 388 fragment types but 0.03/
   1,000 in use (modern text unpointed); Arabic few types but 4.57 (MSA tanwin);
   Hindi worst in use (7.86). And bytes_cb == script_cb with IDENTICAL violation
   counts in all 12 languages: once merges are constrained, the encoding stops
   mattering.
4. THE EXTENSION (RQ3.3, _cbg): 54-line merge constraint (grapheme-cluster rules),
   28 unit tests, old caches untouched. Preregistered projections: Thai 2.82->0.35
   (predicted 0.33), Punjabi 4.82->0.46 (predicted 0.48), ~zero compression cost.
   Arabic 3.16 = measured CEILING of any type-level rule (word-final marks stranded
   as single-char tokens) — this is precisely where only pretokenization could go
   further, which is WHY it's out of scope and cited as future work, not a gap.
5. THE INSTRUCTIVE FAILURE: Hindi with the full rule set went to 111.5/1,000 (14x
   WORSE). Mechanism found same day: a conjunct क्ष needs two merges; both possible
   intermediates ([क्] and [्ष]) were banned by two individually-reasonable rules ->
   clusters became UNREACHABLE. The census's "fragment" types were partly load-
   bearing scaffolding. Dropping the virama rule: 2.84/1,000 (-64% vs _cb) at zero
   cost — second preregistered prediction, hit. Design lesson: type-level rules
   interact; always verify a complete cluster stays reachable.
